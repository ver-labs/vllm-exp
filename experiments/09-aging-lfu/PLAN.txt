Experiment 09: allocation-clock aging of cached-prefix popularity
================================================================

Compare stock eviction, unchanged cumulative LFU and aging LFU independently
of queue retention, partial caching and skip/continue. Qwen3-0.6B pinned at
c1899de289a04d12100db370d81485cdf75e47ca; native Mac Metal GPU.

Predeclared interval:64 allocated physical blocks per half-life, about two
turnovers of the32-page pool. Before victim selection, advance the global
allocation clock by the requested page count and right-shift every reuse count
by all elapsed epochs. All pages use the same epoch, including active pages;
only ref_count=0 pages are eviction candidates. No references/hashes change
when scores decay. Uncached-first, deferred-reader priority and stable free-list
ties are identical to existing LFU. Score0 pages may be evicted normally.
No wall-clock decay or interval tuning based on benchmark outcomes.

Shift workload:12 initial requests reuse document A. Then8 cycles each contain
four document-B training requests, four one-off scan prompts, and one B probe.
84 sequential requests,8 output tokens each=672 outputs. Documents A/B are
padded to256 chat tokens before varying questions; B's full shared prefix is
240 tokens. Adaptation is the first cycle whose probe and next cycle's probe
both reuse the full prefix. Report the entire per-cycle series, not just this
summary. Null means no sustained full-hit pair within the measured window.

Stable control: the existing experiment02 temporal sequence of42 requests
(336 output tokens) with an unchanged hot document. No traffic shift; tests
whether aging destroys useful scan resistance. Same request text as archived02.

All policies/profiles:32 physical pages (31 usable),16-token pages/hash units,
context384, batch128,1 running slot, FCFS, synchronous chunked prefill,
temperature0, seed0, ignore EOS, identical one-token warmup outside timing.
Historical experiment02 used3 slots; these are fresh within-profile comparisons.

One traced diagnostic per policy/profile validates decay events, zero-ref
evictions, API/trace cache-hit agreement and no preemption. Three untraced rounds
per policy with fresh servers and rotated order.24 serving runs total.
Record adaptation, cache hits, phase/probe TTFT and request latency, full batch
latency, throughput, all paired timings and answer agreement. Report negative
results and tradeoffs without retuning the predeclared64-block interval.

Run from the experiment root:
  .venv/bin/python experiments/09-aging-lfu/test_aging_lfu.py
  .venv/bin/python src/run_aging_lfu.py --profile shift --rounds 3
  .venv/bin/python src/run_aging_lfu.py --profile stable --rounds 3
