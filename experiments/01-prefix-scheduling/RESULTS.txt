Experiment 01: prefix-aware and fixed-FCFS-head scheduling (COMPLETED)

The final A-Y comparison used three timed runs per policy, identical inputs,
1600 candidate output tokens per run, and a fresh server and warmup each time.

Policy         Median A-Y time   A first text   B first text
FCFS                  18.77 s         3.37 s         3.40 s
Prefix-first          18.89 s        10.79 s        10.77 s
Hybrid                19.01 s         3.32 s         3.34 s

The hybrid improved A/B latency relative to prefix-first by protecting their
place in the queue. It did not establish an overall advantage over FCFS.
Later requests can wait longer when others are prioritized. Timing differences
between policies remain small relative to variation; three runs are limited.
