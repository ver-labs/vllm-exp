Interpretation limits
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Initial cache contents are seeded, but eviction and closed-loop arrivals affect
which prefixes remain available. Do not require or assume equal cache-hit counts
across runs. All diagnostic per-request lookup hits matched API usage.
Copy counts match fractional prefix hits; all recorded evictions have zero refs.
Diagnostics confirmed running overlap and scheduler waiting. Traces are excluded
from timing comparisons. Output lengths are fixed, so throughput is comparable.
Dense batching and longer decode can dilute a small prefill gain. Compare full
completion time, throughput and tail latency, not isolated first-token wins.
Three rounds are exploratory; per-round times, negative findings and answer
agreement are preserved. Exact text disagreement is not a semantic-quality score.
No new default threshold, scheduling policy or deployment was adopted.
Reproduce:PLAN.txt. Raw logs are indexed by evidence/raw-sha256.json.

Additional scope and quality notes
-----------------------------------------------------------------
All measured target answers matched stock exactly in every paired round.
This does not independently score semantic quality. Prompt lengths, output
limits and divergence offsets are fixed examples, not a fully crossed
factorial design; cohort/length breakdowns are descriptive.
Unrestricted and minimum8 had nearly identical group-median batch completion.
At12 clients unrestricted p95 total latency increased about4.8%, despite a
small batch/throughput gain; no across-the-board latency win is established.

Observed outcome
-----------------------------------------------------------------
C4/partial: batch group-median ratio -3.46%; median TTFT -9.86%; p95 request -2.61%. Negative=faster. Paired batch faster in 2/3 rounds.
C4/minimum8: batch group-median ratio -3.41%; median TTFT -21.59%; p95 request -5.87%. Negative=faster. Paired batch faster in 3/3 rounds.
C12/partial: batch group-median ratio -1.90%; median TTFT -0.45%; p95 request +4.81%. Negative=faster. Paired batch faster in 2/3 rounds.
C12/minimum8: batch group-median ratio -2.00%; median TTFT +1.73%; p95 request +0.02%. Negative=faster. Paired batch faster in 2/3 rounds.
Cache capacity was deliberately fixed; substantial cache eviction can remove
the interior block before a related request arrives. These measurements combine
concurrency, mixed lengths, longer decoding and finite-cache effects; they do not
isolate which of those factors changed the isolated-probe result.
A roomier-cache control or open-loop arrival trace would be separate follow-ups.
All evidence checksums verified; all experimental servers stopped.
