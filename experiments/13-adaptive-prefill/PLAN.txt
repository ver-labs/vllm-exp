Experiment13: adaptive prefill under fixed-time prompt bursts
==========================================================

Three policies: stock chunked prefill threshold0; built-in adaptive
threshold16 with fair-share floor; study decoder-load cap.
Study cap per prompt/request/step = max(16,128//(1+2*decoders)),
clamped to128. An unfinished prompt older than750ms raises global
cap to at least64. Age is since request arrival, not since last progress.
This is not an aggregate prefill cap and not strict decode-first ordering.
Upstream running traversal/FCFS admission and allocation checks are unchanged.
No strict starvation bound: slots/memory/total token budget still constrain work.
Policy is opt-in and mutually exclusive with built-in adaptive and skip/continue.

Four64-token prompts start at0s and generate128 tokens each. Ten prompts
arrive at0.8s, ten at2.4s; each has512/1024 prompt tokens and16 outputs.
24 HTTP workers avoid closed-loop arrival coupling. Prompt tokens and
scheduled arrival times identical across policies; dispatch lateness recorded.
Eight running slots,128-token batch budget,1024 physical16-token pages,
context1280, synchronous scheduler, native Metal worker, no prefix caching,
no other study policies, temperature0/seed0/ignoreEOS. Total832 outputs/run.
One short warmup outside timing. Fresh sequential server per run.
Three traced mechanism diagnostics, then three untraced rotated rounds/policy.
Diagnostics must show mixed prefill+decode steps, queueing and no preemptions;
adaptive cap must change and age boost activate. All requests must complete.
Capture total duration, throughput, cohort TTFT/latency, per-request TPOT
(first-to-last text arrival divided by output count-1), SSE text-chunk gaps,
dispatch lag, exact answer agreement. Chunk gaps are not exact per-token ITL.
No semantic quality claim from text matching. Three rounds exploratory.
Repeated timing results are local synthetic workload evidence, not universal.
Compare to tuned fixed thresholds as future work before superiority claims.
