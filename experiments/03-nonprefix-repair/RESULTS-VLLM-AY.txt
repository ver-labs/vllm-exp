statistical significance or a general advantage. Do not label this a win.

The reuse path actually ran in every diagnostic/timed repair run:22 physical
source pages,352 matching tokens,44 repaired rows,308 skipped rows per layer.
The10% requested boundary ratio rounds to2/16 rows (12.5% actual repair/page).

already reused2432 candidate tokens in both variants. Matching middle-page
lookup adds CPU work; hit steps gather KV and use separate dense SDPA segments,
which can outweigh the skipped projection/attention/MLP rows. The scheduler
still accounts for the original token positions and allocates normal KV blocks.
