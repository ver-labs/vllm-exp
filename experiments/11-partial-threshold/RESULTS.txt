Results measure target first-text/total latency and source+target request time.
Pair time includes source publication, excludes cache resets and startup.
Min16 is same-granularity recomputation control; stock16 measures total feature cost.
All observed cached tokens match predictions; copies equal accepted partial hits.
No preemptions. Rejected hits retain full-block reuse and do not copy.

Interpretation and limits
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Additional saved tokens refer only to the partial block, beyond112 full-block hits.
Granularity rounds down savings; minimum8 rejects a rounded gain below8.
g8/min0 and g8/min8 have identical reuse decisions: their spread is a noise check.
Look for repeated wins across offsets and rounds, not one best point.
Three server rounds with three repeats each are exploratory. Paired ratios compare
corresponding round/repetition, not simultaneously running servers. Launch order
rotates but is not fully balanced over all10 configurations in only3 rounds.
No single measured offset establishes a universal crossover or optimum threshold.
Exact causal KV reuse can change low-precision prefill shapes; text agreement is
reported separately and does not establish semantic quality.
These are synthetic one-output-token latency probes, not production concurrency
or long-output throughput measurements. Reproduction:PLAN.txt.

Observed outcome on this workload
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Unrestricted one-token matching: target first text16.610ms versus stock19.666ms
(15.5% lower); source+target40.073ms versus43.242ms (7.3% lower).
Eight-token minimum with one-token matching: first text19.137ms, worse than
unrestricted16.610ms; this threshold removed useful small hits.
At one-token saving, unrestricted matching beat same-g recomputation in8/9
paired probes; median target18.549ms versus20.046ms. This is the smallest
tested gain and not a universal break-even estimate.
Four-token unrestricted matching was close overall (16.692ms); eight-token
matching was slower (about18ms) but still beat stock in the overall median.
All1350 timed target texts match corresponding same-g controls exactly.
First encounters of prefill shapes can include warmup effects; repeated probes
help but these are not exhaustive steady-state/production benchmarks.
No automatic choice or new global minimum was adopted. Default minimum stays0.
