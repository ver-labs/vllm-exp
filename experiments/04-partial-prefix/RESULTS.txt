Experiment04: partial-prefix caching against the original vLLM baseline
=====================================================================

Implemented and benchmarked 2026-10-21. Reuse through an interior prefix works:
in the32-token GPU probe, baseline reused16 tokens and the new mode reused31.
Eight generated tokens matched a cold recomputation in all three variants.
Six allocation/configuration tests pass, including the original7-of8 example,
source retention, eviction/reset, promotion, and deferred-free cleanup.

The original concurrent A-Y manifest was reused unchanged. Same Qwen3-0.6B
checkpoint, Metal GPU,96 physical blocks,16 tokens/block,3 running slots,
128 batch tokens,512 context, FCFS, upstream eviction and native Metal worker.
Experiment03's approximate reuse/repair is disabled. No new persistent KV pool.
25 candidates generate64 tokens each;3 blockers generate128 each (1984 total).
Three fresh-server timing rounds per variant, rotated order. Traced diagnostics
are separate from timings. Startup is excluded; hash/index/copy costs included.

The median A-Y completion time is0.64% lower and throughput0.64% higher.
Partial was slightly faster than baseline in all3 rounds, but the effect is
small, timing ranges overlap, and3 rounds do not establish significance or a
general speedup. Partial beat the finer-hash-only control in2/3 rounds; that
comparison also has substantial variation. Keep this as a small observed gain.

Actual reuse increased by131 candidate tokens/run (2432 ->2563), consistently
across every diagnostic/timing run. Diagnostic examples:

  C:176 ->189 cached tokens
  D:192 ->192 cached tokens
  I:16 ->22 cached tokens
  Y:176 ->191 cached tokens
There were23 A-Y CoW page copies in the partial diagnostic,0 in both controls.
All diagnostics observed25 candidates waiting and admission orderA-Y, with0
preemptions. All12 serving runs were valid; all28 requests succeeded per run.

Output comparison:
  Fine-hash-only:25/25 candidate answers identical to baseline in every round.
  Partial:16/25 identical in every round;9 differ (C,I,J,K,M,P,Q,S,V).
  Each variant's answers were stable across its three repeats.
The algorithm reuses only matching full-prefix KV; no stale-context approximation
or selective repair is used. Different prefill chunk shapes and low-precision
execution can change generated text. This run does not isolate the cause of
every text difference or score semantic quality; do not claim bitwise output
identity or quality preservation from these results. The targeted31/32 probe
and ownership tests support correct partial-cache operation, not universal quality.
