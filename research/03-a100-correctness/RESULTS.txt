A100 model correctness results — 24 October 2026
================================================

Outcome
-------
Both pinned Qwen models passed all five arms in the final matrix, with
VLLM_BATCH_INVARIANT=1, BF16 and FlashAttention 2 on one A100.
All 3,144 candidate-versus-stock vocabulary-logit vectors were also independently
confirmed byte-identical. Ten gathered full-prompt KV cold/hot tensor pairs
were byte-identical. This is bounded correctness, not serving-speed or quality.

What passed
-----------
Cold recomputation versus prefix reuse; full logits under identical forced
continuations; separate greedy output-ID agreement; different-context and
tenant-salt misses; actual copy-value checks in the runner; gathered KV values
versus recomputation across every layer; cached-block eviction; aging epochs;
cancellation after a model step; failed and forced live reset; memory-pressure
preemption; stored/output token preservation; and no leaked block references.

Controls and limits
-------------------
The original default non-invariant BF16 stock control exceeded the fixed logit
tolerance (including max absolute error 0.75). The later invariant matrix does
not erase this failed gate or establish parity in the default deployment mode.
A separate built-in batch-invariant control passed without relaxing tolerances.
Two earlier failures were harness assumptions: legacy runner access and
requiring positive CoW refs in a valid synchronous execution window. Original
code versions, failures and corrected ordering checks are preserved.

No frozen engine source was modified. No inference performance measurements
were taken. Controlled prompts and short greedy checks do not establish
semantic quality, broad workload reliability, long-context or asynchronous/
distributed correctness. Read PROTOCOL.txt for exact settings and boundaries.

Evidence
--------
Per-arm JSON, logs, comparisons, model/tokenizer pins, source helpers and
checksums are versioned here. The complete raw logits/KV archive is preserved
on the GPU cluster; its outer hash and every raw member hash were verified
there. A compact archive, selected full vocabulary vectors and selected KV
values are stored locally under ignored .artifacts, with checksums verified
here. The full large archive was not downloaded over the slow connection.
See raw-evidence-storage.json for exact locations and hashes. Raw archives are
not included in a fresh clone; public archival deposition is separate future
work. Older frozen research-package receipts remain unchanged.
