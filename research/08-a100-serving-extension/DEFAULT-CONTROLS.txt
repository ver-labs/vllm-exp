Untimed default-mode controls — 3 November 2026
=============================================

These explanatory controls were added after the first numerical failures and
before performance measurement. They do not reverse the preregistered timing
exclusions. One fresh stock repeat and one same-source feature-disabled server
were run per model on the identical saved inputs, with graphs enabled.

Cold four-step full-distribution check:
  Stock versus stock-repeat: all32 API log-probability vectors exactly equal
    for each of the three models (96/96 in total).
  Stock versus features-off: both Qwen models differ, including prefill;
    SmolLM3 is equal in all32 vectors.
  Features-off versus optimized: prefill vectors are equal in every model.
    Decode differences remain: 23/32 total vectors exactly equal for each Qwen
    size, 20/32 for SmolLM3. Same-history maximum RMS is .1798, .2803 and .1007,
    respectively. Every model fails the fixed API distribution limits.

This separates two observations. Qwen's cross-source prefill differences cannot
be attributed solely to a guard active only at single-query decode. Their
underlying source/compilation cause is not isolated further. However, the
same-source flag comparison independently shows default-mode decode numerical
differences, so the default-mode exclusion is not explained away by that
cross-source confound.

Natural-response exact token-sequence matches out of eight pairs:
                              Qwen0.6B   Qwen1.7B   SmolLM3

Variable batching/arrivals can change default-mode greedy sequences even when
repeating stock. These small, unlabelled natural-response samples are not
task-accuracy scores or an estimate of a general quality regression. Full
per-step diagnostics distinguish matched histories from histories that have
already diverged. No speed claims are made from these untimed controls.

The validated batch-invariant graph mode remains exactly equal for all96
checked full distributions and all24 natural response pairs. Only that mode
is admitted to the primary six-repeat latency/throughput matrix.
