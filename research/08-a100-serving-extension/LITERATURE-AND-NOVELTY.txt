Literature and upstream review — 3 November 2026
===============================================

Finding
-------
The copying problem and the packed-GQA decode path are known. Our existing
evidence supports a reproducible performance case study of a guarded routing
choice on a frozen vLLM/FA2 stack. It does not establish a new attention
algorithm, a new KV-cache algorithm, priority over prior work, or applicability
to all current serving defaults.

Closest implementation work
---------------------------
1. Dao-AILab/flash-attention PR3066, opened 21 October 2026, proposes writing a
   caller-provided output buffer directly on the same varlen single-query GQA
   path. It explicitly identifies the output reshape copy and changes output
   strides while retaining grouped-query packing. Our change instead avoids
   the packed path under a narrow guard, so it also changes the compute route.
   This is close prior work and an important alternative comparator. Its
   reported GPU results are the author's claims, not measurements reproduced
   in our repository. It was open when inspected. No duplicate PR is proposed.
   https://github.com/Dao-AILab/flash-attention/pull/3066

2. The original vLLM-fork PR32 and full commit
   04325b6798bcc326c86fb35af62d05a9c8c8eceb were independently fetched. It merged
   on 2 December 2024 and explicitly documents the redundant reshape clone,
   uses a temporary output, then copies into the caller's output view. Our
   pinned source already contains this copy-back implementation. This
   establishes that the issue predates our study; we must not claim discovery
   of the copy. The full original commit metadata/diff is saved in upstream/.
   https://github.com/vllm-project/flash-attention/pull/32
   https://github.com/vllm-project/flash-attention/commit/04325b6798bcc326c86fb35af62d05a9c8c8eceb

3. vllm-project/flash-attention PR87 fixes LSE layout in a split-KV case. This
   concerns correctness of regrouped softmax statistics, not our end-to-end
   routing performance claim. It motivates retaining LSE/reference tests.
   https://github.com/vllm-project/flash-attention/pull/87

4. vllm-project/vllm PR61129 caches transpose/split tensor views of the KV store.
   Its claimed target is repeated view construction, not materializing the
   single-query Q/output layout copies measured in phase05. Related overhead
   work should be cited rather than conflated with our mechanism.
   https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/61129

Source status
-------------
Current vLLM main was frozen at a900ac810ecaedc0c2c5e9a8887e353cf1a5c350.
Its CMake dependency is b273a02621d3c94bc747df67204f591b67f38fae in the
vllm-project FlashAttention fork. That exact dependency retains the one-query
head-group transpose/reshape and output-copy logic; main of the fork retains
the mechanism too. The source describes packing as an intended optimization.
Presence of this code does not prove that a selected modern backend executes
it, nor that our patch wins with current native binaries. Those require actual
dispatch and timing evidence. Our GPU executable remains the frozen v0.31.0
source/native stack; current-main runtime compatibility has not been tested.

Immutable source URLs, fetched contents, commit metadata, SHA256s and query
responses are saved in upstream/. Broad searches are paginated only to the
first100 results and are explicitly incomplete. Exact-symbol queries are more
focused. An absence in those results would not prove novelty.

Research context
----------------
GQA (Ainslie et al., EMNLP2023) reduces the KV-head count between MHA and MQA.
We retain pretrained models and do not propose or train a new attention form.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.13245

FlashAttention-2 (Dao, ICLR2024) optimizes attention parallelism and work
partitioning. We reuse its native implementation rather than introduce a new
attention kernel.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.08691

Flash-Decoding (Dao, Haziza, Massa, Sizov, 2023) parallelizes decoding over KV
chunks; FlashDecoding++ (Hong et al., 2023/2024) studies softmax, flat GEMMs and
hardware-dependent dataflow. These establish that decode execution choices
and their hardware/shape tradeoffs are existing research areas. Their reported
speedups use different stacks and cannot be compared numerically to ours.
https://pytorch.org/blog/flash-decoding/
https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.01282

PagedAttention (Kwon et al., SOSP2023) manages paged KV storage and sharing.
Our isolated attention experiments leave cache policy unchanged, so the
attention-copy result must not be framed as a new cache eviction/reuse method.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.06180

Claim we can currently defend
----------------------------
On the declared frozen A100 BF16 batch-invariant eager FA2 configuration, a
guarded bypass of the packed one-query GQA path reduced measured copies and
improved throughput in the tested Qwen workloads. The longer-prompt/concurrency
sweep showed about1.8–8.0% improvement with matching generated IDs; TTFT gains
were not consistent. This is an empirical implementation result with explicit
limits. A publication should explain why the existing fast path loses here,
compare close alternatives, and report cases where the bypass is neutral or
slower. The separate graph/human-prompt extension now measures three models
in two families: its conditional gains and high-load regressions are recorded
in INTERPRETATION.txt. Default BF16 comparisons failed the declared numerical
gate. Task-level accuracy and current-upstream runtime remain unvalidated.

Publication assessment
----------------------
An engineering article/case study is already plausible with the existing
reproducible evidence and proper prior-work attribution. A research paper's
novelty is not established by the review. A stronger research contribution
could be a measured, general cost model or adaptive choice between packed and
ordinary decode paths, evaluated against both current default backends and
the direct-output alternative on held-out shapes/hardware. That is a possible
future question, not a completed contribution or promised acceptance.
